HOW TO PLAY

  • GREEN Touch it. Worth 100 × your multiplier. Each green raises the multiplier by 1, up to ×8. The ring around the green shows your combo time left and the ×value of the next one. If the ring runs out, the multiplier resets. A shield hit halves it.
  • DASH Space or click.Tap DASH. -25 points, refunded if it breaks a drone. A miss costs one multiplier step, but never resets your combo timer.
  • RED Stay away. A hit costs one shield. Each wave sends a fixed number of drones. A red marker on the edge warns you 0.6 seconds before one appears. Drones fade away after about 9 seconds, and a short calm between waves clears the box.
  • SHARD A broken drone drops a gold shard. Grab it for +15 points. Shards are never multiplied.
  • GRAZE Skim close to a red drone without touching it for +10 points, up to 3 times per drone.
  • MODES ARCADE is the normal endless mode. BLITZ is a 90 second run with a new twist every wave. Pick one with the buttons above the arena before you start. Only ARCADE counts for the leaderboard. Blitz keeps its own best on this device.
  • SHAPES Diamonds drift. Arrows chase you. Spiked cores are slow. In Blitz, triangles snake side to side, big hexagons are slow but wide, thin needles creep then lunge, and sizes vary a little. All red shapes cost a shield.
  • LAST CHANCE At 0 shields, time slows once per wave when a drone is about to hit you.
  • BLITZ A 90 second timed run. Every wave brings a new twist, shown as a banner: SEEKER SWARM, DRIFTER RAIN, SPIKE FIELD, MOVING PRISM, WEAVER WAVE or TITAN WAVE. Every 5th wave adds a big boss drone. Its best score is kept separate from arcade.
  • OUTER INNER You have 2 shield rings. The violet outer ring breaks first, then the lime inner ring. A hit with no ring left ends the run. Both rings come back at every new wave.
  • GHOST Fills from greens, grazes and broken drones. It cannot refill while it is running. Press E or wheel click when full.Tap GHOST when full.
  • Move with mouse, WASD or arrows. P to pause.Move with the stick. Use the button under it to switch to arrows.
